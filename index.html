Four frames: why the call comes before the payment

Same swarm, same USDC, same x402 handshake. The only difference is whether your agent knew who it was paying.

x402 as intended: your agent sends USDC on Base, the seller sends back what it sold. No account, no invoice, two seconds.

Most sellers deliver. A few never do — and before the payment they look exactly the same: an address, a price, a promise.

The USDC leaves, nothing comes back. x402 has no chargeback: the address earns its label only after it has your money.

One call, one cent, before the money moves. We score the address on its whole history on Base — your agent pays the sellers that deliver and holds back from the ones that don't.
